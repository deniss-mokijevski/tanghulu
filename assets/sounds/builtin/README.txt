The game's built-in Estonian voice (made with tools/make-voice.js).
Kõnesüntees: Neurokõne (TartuNLP, Tartu Ülikool), hääl Mari. https://neurokone.ee

Don't put your own recordings here: they go in ../letters, ../words and ../voice,
and they always win over these clips. A clip is only used while its words still
match config.js. To make new ones after changing words: node tools/make-voice.js
